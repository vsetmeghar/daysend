Day's End privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Day's End is a personal journal that runs in your web browser. This page explains what happens to the information you write in it.

What the app stores

The answers you type are saved in your own browser, on your own device. The person who runs this website does not receive them and cannot see them. There is no account to create and no server that holds your entries.

Google Doc sync (optional)

If you choose "Connect a Google Doc", the app asks Google for permission to create and edit files that the app itself creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It uses this to create one Google Doc called "Day's End Journal" and to write your journal entries into it. Your entries go straight from your browser to Google's Docs service. They do not pass through any server run by the operator of this app. The app cannot see or open any other file in your Google Drive.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the journal sync you asked for. It is not sold, shared with other parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Other services the page loads

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and, only if sync is switched on, Google's sign-in script. Google may receive your IP address and browser details when these load, as it does for any website that uses them.

Your choices

To delete your entries from the device, clear this site's data in your browser settings. To delete the Google Doc, delete it in Google Drive. To stop the app writing to Google, remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Day's End does not keep any copy of your entries on its own servers, so there is nothing further to delete.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone. Entries stay on the device that created them unless that person turns on Google Doc sync with their own Google account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's page at github.com/vsetmeghar/daysend/issues.
